FAQ
Good questions.

Do I have to pay anything upfront?

No. We photograph your space, create your listings, and manage everything at our own cost. You only pay our 12.5% fee when a confirmed booking takes place.

What about my existing customers?

Completely unaffected. Our fee only applies to new bookings that come through the platforms we manage. Walk-ins, regulars, and direct enquiries are 100% yours.

Can I say no to a booking?

Absolutely. You approve every booking before it's confirmed. We never commit your space without your say-so.

How much time does it take from me?

About 30 minutes for the initial photography visit. After that, just responding when we check your availability — usually a quick text or email.

What if it doesn't work?

Then you've lost nothing. No setup costs, no monthly fees. You can cancel anytime with 30 days' notice. We take on all the risk.

Which venues do you work with?

Pubs with function rooms, restaurants with private dining rooms, community halls, and unique event spaces across London. If you've got a room people can hire, we'd love to chat.
